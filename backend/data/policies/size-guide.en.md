# Size guide

## How to measure yourself
Use a soft tape measure and measure over light clothing. For the chest, measure around the fullest part. For the waist, measure around the narrowest part of your torso. For the hips, measure around the fullest part. Keep the tape level and snug, without squeezing.

## Tops and jackets sizes
Chest measurements: XS is 80 to 86 cm, S is 87 to 94 cm, M is 95 to 102 cm, L is 103 to 110 cm and XL is 111 to 118 cm. Choose the size that matches your chest measurement.

## Trousers and skirts sizes
Waist measurements: XS is 62 to 68 cm, S is 69 to 76 cm, M is 77 to 84 cm, L is 85 to 92 cm and XL is 93 to 100 cm. Hip measurements are 6 to 8 cm larger than the waist for a standard fit.

## Shoe sizes
Measure your foot from heel to longest toe, standing, in centimetres. A foot length of 23.0 cm matches size 36, 24.0 cm matches size 38, 25.0 cm matches size 40, 26.0 cm matches size 42, 27.0 cm matches size 44. Shoe sizes in the shop are given in this European scale.

## Between two sizes
If your measurements are between two sizes, choose the larger size for a comfortable fit. For a close fit, choose the smaller one. For shoes, choose the larger size if you wear thick socks.

## Fit of our items
Items fit true to size unless the product page says otherwise, for example "slim fit" or "oversized". Always read the fit note on the product page.

## Stretch and fabric
Items with elastane stretch and are more forgiving. Items in 100% cotton or linen have little stretch. Wool items can fit slightly closer after washing if the care label is not followed.

## If the size is wrong
If an item does not fit, you can ask for an exchange for another size, depending on stock, or return it for a refund, within 30 days after delivery and if the item is unworn with its tag.

## Underwear and swimwear sizing
Underwear and swimwear follow the same chest, waist and hip measurements. Because they cannot be returned or exchanged for hygiene reasons, check your size carefully before ordering.
