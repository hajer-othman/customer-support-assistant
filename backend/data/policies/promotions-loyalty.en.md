# Promotions and loyalty programme

## Promo codes
A promo code is entered at checkout before payment. Only one promo code can be used per order. A code has its own conditions, such as an expiry date or a minimum amount, shown with the offer.

## Sale prices
Items on sale are sold at the displayed reduced price. Sale items follow the same return and refund rules as other items, except underwear and swimwear which cannot be returned.

## Refund of discounted orders
When you return an item bought with a discount or a promo code, you are refunded the amount you actually paid for that item, not its full price.

## Loyalty programme
The loyalty programme is free and open to everyone with a customer account. You earn 1 point for each euro spent on items, delivery fees not included.

## Using your points
100 points are worth a 5 EUR discount on a future order. Your points balance is shown in your customer account, and you choose to use points at checkout.

## Points expiry
Points expire 12 months after the day they were earned. Unused points disappear after this period.

## Points and returns
When an item is returned and refunded, the points earned for that item are removed from your balance. If you used points as a discount on an item you return, the refund follows the amount actually paid.

## Welcome offer
New customers receive a welcome promo code by email after creating an account. The code has its own conditions and expiry date.

## Newsletter and offers
You can receive offers by email if you agree to it. You can unsubscribe at any time from the link at the bottom of any email.
