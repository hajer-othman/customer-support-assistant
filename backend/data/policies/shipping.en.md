# Delivery

## Standard delivery time
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## Express delivery time
Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. Choose express delivery at checkout when you want your order faster.

## Delivery costs
Standard delivery costs 4.90 EUR when the amount of your items is under 50 EUR, and it is free from 50 EUR: an order of exactly 50 EUR in items qualifies. Express delivery costs 7 EUR whatever the amount of the order: express delivery is never free.

## How the free delivery threshold is calculated
The 50 EUR threshold applies to the amount of the items in your order, delivery fees not included. Adding delivery fees to the total does not help you reach the threshold.

## Where we deliver
The shop delivers to a postal address within the country where it operates. International delivery is not offered.

## Order statuses
An order goes through several statuses. "Processing" means the shop is preparing it. "Shipped" means the parcel has left the shop. "Delivered" means the parcel has arrived. You can ask the assistant for the status of your order at any time.

## Choosing the delivery address
You enter your delivery address when you place the order. You can change it only while the order is still in preparation, see the cancellation and address change policy.

## When your order is delivered
Once the order is marked as delivered, the 30-day return period starts. See the return policy for the conditions.

## Late or lost parcels
If your standard order has not arrived after 5 business days, or if the parcel is marked as delivered but you did not receive it, see the order tracking and delivery incidents policy. The assistant can pass the case to an advisor.
