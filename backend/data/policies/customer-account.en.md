# Customer account

## Creating an account
You can create a customer account with your email address and a password. An account lets you see your orders, follow your requests and use the loyalty programme.

## Logging in
Customers log in with their email address and password from the login page. After logging in, you are taken to your customer space.

## Ordering and asking questions without an account
You can ask the assistant about an order without logging in by giving your order number and the email address used for the order. The assistant only shows information about an order that matches both.

## Forgotten password
Use the "forgot password" link on the login page. A reset link is sent to your email address. If you do not receive it, check your spam folder, then tell the assistant.

## Changing your details
You can change your name, email address and delivery addresses in your customer account. Changing the details of an existing order, including its delivery address, follows the cancellation and address change policy.

## Order history
Your customer account shows your orders, their status and your invoices. You can also ask the assistant for the status of an order or of a request.

## Account security
Choose a password that you do not use elsewhere. Never share your password, not even with the assistant or an advisor: they will never ask for it.

## Email not received
If a confirmation or reset email does not arrive, check your spam folder and the spelling of your email address. If you still cannot find it, tell the assistant and it creates a ticket.

## Deleting your account
To delete your account, tell the assistant. It creates a ticket for an advisor who handles the request. Loyalty points are lost when an account is deleted.
