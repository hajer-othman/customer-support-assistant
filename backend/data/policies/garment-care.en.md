# Garment care

## Always read the care label
Every item has a care label sewn inside. It gives the washing temperature, drying, ironing and cleaning instructions. Follow it first. The advice below is general and applies when a label is missing or unclear.

## Cotton and linen
Wash cotton and linen at 30 to 40 degrees with similar colours. Wash dark and bright colours separately for the first washes. Dry flat or at low heat. Iron while slightly damp for linen.

## Wool and knitwear
Wash wool by hand in cold water with a gentle detergent, or on the wool cycle. Do not wring. Press the water out with a towel and dry flat, away from direct heat. Hanging a wet pullover makes it lose its shape.

## Synthetic fabrics
Polyester, nylon and similar fabrics wash at 30 degrees and dry quickly. Avoid high heat in the dryer and high iron temperatures, which can damage the fibres.

## Denim
Wash jeans inside out at 30 degrees, and not too often, to keep the colour. Dark denim can bleed on the first washes, so wash it alone.

## Leather and suede shoes
Wipe leather shoes with a damp cloth and apply a leather care product. Brush suede shoes with a suede brush and use a protective spray. Never put leather or suede shoes in a washing machine.

## Canvas and sport shoes
Remove the laces, brush off the dust, and clean with lukewarm water and mild soap. Let them dry at room temperature, away from a radiator.

## Removing stains
Treat a stain quickly with cold water. Do not rub, dab gently. Avoid hot water on stains of blood, coffee or sauces because heat can set them.

## Storage
Store clothes clean and dry. Fold knitwear instead of hanging it. Keep shoes stuffed with paper to keep their shape.

## Care and returns
Washing or wearing an item means it can no longer be returned, because returns require an unworn item with its tag. Try an item on carefully, without wearing it outside, if you may return it.
