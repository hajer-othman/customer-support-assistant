# Defective or non-conforming products

## What counts as a defect
A defective item has a manufacturing fault: a seam that comes apart, a broken zip or sole, a fabric flaw or a colour fault that was there from the start. Normal wear and tear is not a defect.

## Deadline to report a defect
A defect must be reported within 30 days after the delivery date of the order. After 30 days, the shop no longer handles the case through the standard procedure, and the assistant passes it to an advisor who decides on any follow-up.

## Exception to the unworn condition
The standard return rule requires an unworn item. For a defect, this is relaxed: an item that has been worn or washed once can still be returned within 30 days if the defect is not caused by misuse. An advisor reviews each case.

## What is not a defect
Damage caused by misuse, accidents, washing or cleaning that does not follow the care label, changes made to the item, or normal wear are not manufacturing defects.

## How to report a defect
Describe the defect in your message to the assistant: which item, which order, when you noticed it and what is wrong. The assistant checks the delivery date and creates a ticket for an advisor. An advisor may ask you for more information by email.

## Who decides
An advisor decides whether the item is defective. The assistant cannot decide this itself.

## Possible solutions
Depending on the case, an advisor can propose a replacement of the same item, depending on stock, or a refund to your original payment method.

## Wrong item received
If you received an item that is not the one you ordered, tell the assistant. It creates a ticket for an advisor who handles the case.

## Damaged during delivery
If the parcel or the item was damaged when it arrived, see the order tracking and delivery incidents policy.

## Costs
Sending back a defective or non-conforming item is free of charge.
