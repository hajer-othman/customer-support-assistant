# Privacy and personal data

## What data the shop keeps
The shop keeps the data needed to run your orders and your customer account: your name, email address, delivery addresses, order history, returns and tickets. It also keeps your conversations with the assistant.

## Why the data is used
Your data is used to process and deliver your orders, handle returns and refunds, answer your requests, and improve the service. It is not sold to third parties.

## Payment data
The shop does not store your full card number. Payments are processed by the payment provider.

## Who your data is shared with
Your data is shared only with the partners needed to serve you, such as the delivery carrier and the payment provider, and only for that purpose.

## Conversations with the assistant
The assistant is an automated system. Your messages are stored with your conversation, and advisors can read them when they handle a ticket created from it. Do not write payment card numbers or passwords in the chat.

## Who can see your information
The assistant only shows you information about your own orders and requests, after your identity has been verified. It never shows another customer's data.

## Your rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Tell the assistant: it creates a ticket for an advisor who handles the request.

## How long data is kept
Order data is kept as long as needed for orders, returns and accounting. When you ask to delete your account, data that no longer needs to be kept is deleted.

## Marketing emails
You receive marketing emails only if you agreed to them. You can unsubscribe at any time from the link at the bottom of an email.
