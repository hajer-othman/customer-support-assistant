# Contact and opening hours

## Ways to contact the shop
You can contact the shop through the chat assistant, which is available at any time, or by email at support@example.com. Advisors do not join the chat live: they handle tickets created from your conversation or from your email.

## Advisor hours
Advisors work Monday to Friday, from 9:00 to 18:00 local time, excluding public holidays. Tickets created outside these hours are handled on the next business day.

## Response time
Advisors usually answer tickets within 2 business days. Complex cases, such as a lost parcel or a payment problem, can take longer. You can ask the assistant for the status of your ticket at any time.

## What the assistant can do
The assistant answers questions about the shop policies, looks up your order or the status of a request, creates a return after checking the rules and getting your confirmation, and passes other requests to an advisor.

## What the assistant cannot do
The assistant cannot modify an existing order, move money, ship parcels or update stock. For refunds, exchanges, cancellations, address changes and payment problems, it creates a ticket for an advisor.

## Information to prepare
To help you faster, have your order number and the email address used for the order ready. Describe your problem in one message with the details: item, date and what you expected.

## Languages
The assistant and the advisors work in French and English. Messages in other languages cannot be handled.

## Complaints
To make a complaint, tell the assistant. It creates a ticket for an advisor, who reviews your case and leaves a note when it is resolved.

## Public holidays
On public holidays, advisors are unavailable. The assistant remains available to answer questions and to create tickets, which are handled on the next business day.
