# Gift cards

## What a gift card is
A gift card is a digital code with a fixed value that can be used to pay for items in the shop. It is sent by email and can be offered to someone else.

## Available amounts
Gift cards are available in amounts of 20 EUR, 50 EUR and 100 EUR.

## How you receive a gift card
After payment, the gift card code is sent to the email address you give when you buy it. It can be sent to yourself or to the person you want to offer it to.

## Using a gift card
Enter the gift card code at checkout, before paying. The value is deducted from your total. Gift cards can be used to pay for items and delivery fees.

## Using a gift card with another payment method
If the order costs more than the card balance, the gift card covers part of it and you pay the rest with another payment method. If the order costs less, the remaining balance stays on the card.

## Validity
A gift card is valid for 12 months after the purchase date. An expired gift card can no longer be used.

## Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged for cash
A gift card cannot be returned, refunded, exchanged for cash or exchanged for another card once it has been purchased.

## Refund of an order paid with a gift card
When an order paid with a gift card is refunded, the amount returns to the balance of the card, because that was the payment method used.

## Lost or unreceived code
If you did not receive the code, check your spam folder first. If it is not there, or if you lost the code, tell the assistant. It creates a ticket for an advisor.
