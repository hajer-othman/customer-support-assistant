# Payment methods

## Accepted payment methods
The shop accepts bank cards (Visa and Mastercard), PayPal, and mobile wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay. Gift cards from the shop can also be used.

## Currency
All prices are shown and charged in euros (EUR).

## When you are charged
Your payment is taken when you validate your order at checkout. You receive a confirmation email once the payment is accepted.

## Payment security
Card payments use strong customer authentication when your bank requires it. The shop does not store your full card number. Payments are processed by the payment provider.

## Payment refused
A payment can be refused for several reasons: wrong card details, insufficient funds, a limit set by your bank or a failed authentication. Check the details and try again, or use another payment method. If the problem continues, tell the assistant and it will pass it to an advisor.

## Charged twice
If you see a double charge, or a payment taken without a confirmed order, tell the assistant. It creates a ticket for an advisor, who handles payment problems. The assistant cannot move money itself.

## Paying in instalments
Payment in instalments is not offered. The full amount is charged when you validate the order.

## Using a promo code or gift card at payment
Enter your promo code or gift card code at checkout before paying. A gift card can cover part of the order, and the rest can be paid with another method.

## Invoices
An invoice is sent with your order confirmation, and you can find your orders in your customer account.

## Refunds and payment methods
Refunds are made to the payment method used for the order. See the refund policy.
