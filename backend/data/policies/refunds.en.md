# Refunds

## Refund method
Refunds are made to the payment method used for the order. A refund cannot be sent to another card or another account.

## Refund timing
The refund is made within 5 to 10 business days after the shop receives your returned parcel. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## When the refund period starts
The 5 to 10 business days are counted from the day the shop receives the parcel, not from the day you request the return, not from the day the return is approved and not from the day you ship it.

## Return approval is not the refund
When an advisor approves a return, it means you can send the item back. The refund itself is processed only after the item is received by the shop.

## Which items are refunded
The refund covers the items you returned. If you return only one item of an order, only that item is refunded.

## How refund requests are handled
The assistant cannot move money. A refund request, or any question about a refund that has not arrived, is passed to an advisor as a ticket. The advisor handles it and resolves the ticket with a note.

## How to check the status of a refund
Ask the assistant for the status of your refund. The status of a refund is the status of its ticket: "open" while an advisor is handling it, "resolved" once the advisor has finished and left a note.

## Refund not received
If more than 10 business days have passed since the shop received your parcel and you have not seen the refund, tell the assistant. It will escalate your case to an advisor. Banks can also take a few extra days to show a refund on your statement.

## Payment problems
A double charge, a payment that was taken but no order appears, or any other payment problem is handled by an advisor. Tell the assistant what happened and it will create a ticket for you.

## Refund of an order paid with a gift card
If your order was paid with a gift card, the refund goes back to the gift card balance, because that was the payment method used.
