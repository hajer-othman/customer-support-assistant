# Order tracking and delivery incidents

## How to track your order
Ask the assistant for the status of your order by giving your order number and the email address used for the order. The assistant tells you whether it is being prepared, shipped or delivered.

## Meaning of the statuses
"Processing" means the shop is preparing your order. "Shipped" means the parcel has left the shop and is on its way. "Delivered" means the parcel has arrived at the delivery address.

## Order still in preparation
Orders are prepared before being shipped. While your order is "processing", it has not left the shop yet, and you can still ask for a cancellation or an address change.

## Parcel late
A standard order normally arrives within 3 to 5 business days, and an express order within 1 to 2 business days. If your order is late compared to these times, tell the assistant: it creates a ticket for an advisor.

## Marked delivered but not received
If the status is "delivered" but you did not receive the parcel, check with neighbours or the people at your address first. If you still cannot find it, tell the assistant and it creates a ticket.

## Lost parcel
If a parcel seems lost, an advisor investigates with the carrier. Depending on the result, the advisor decides on the solution. The assistant cannot ship a new parcel or move money itself.

## Damaged parcel or item
If the parcel or the item arrives damaged, tell the assistant and describe the damage. It creates a ticket for an advisor. Within 30 days, you can also use the return procedure if the item is unworn.

## Missing item in the parcel
If an item of your order is missing from the parcel, tell the assistant which one. It creates a ticket for an advisor.

## Information to give in a ticket
Give your order number, the item concerned, the date you expected the parcel and what you observed. The more precise you are, the faster an advisor can help.

## Following your ticket
Ask the assistant for the status of your ticket: "open" while an advisor handles it, "resolved" when the advisor leaves a note.
